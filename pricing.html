FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

Can I change my plan at any time?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan instantly from your dashboard. When upgrading, you'll only pay the prorated difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle, and you'll receive account credit for the difference.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

We'll never surprise you with overage charges. When you approach your limits, we'll notify you with upgrade suggestions. If you exceed limits, we'll temporarily throttle performance rather than charge extra, giving you time to upgrade or optimize your usage.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes! Save 20% when you pay annually. For example, our Professional plan costs $79/month when billed monthly, but only $63/month when billed annually. Enterprise customers can also access custom annual contracts with additional savings.

Is there a free trial available?

Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial - no credit card required. You'll have full access to all features during your trial. We'll send gentle reminders before your trial ends, and you can cancel anytime with zero commitment.

What's included in the Enterprise plan?

Enterprise includes everything in Professional plus: unlimited API calls, dedicated account manager, custom integrations, advanced security features, SLA guarantees, priority support, and custom onboarding. Pricing starts at $299/month with volume discounts available.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not completely satisfied within your first month, we'll provide a full refund - no questions asked. We're confident you'll love TechVision, but we want you to feel secure in your investment.
