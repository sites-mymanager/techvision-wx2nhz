Our Story

Born from Frustration, Driven by Vision

In 2019, our founder Sarah Chen was working as a CTO at a Fortune 500 company, watching brilliant ideas die in endless committee meetings while outdated systems held back innovation. She saw talented teams burning out, trying to force modern solutions through legacy infrastructure that simply wasn't built for today's pace of change.

That's when TechVision was born—not in a garage or dorm room, but in the quiet frustration of knowing there had to be a better way. We started with a simple belief: technology should amplify human potential, not constrain it. Our first client was a healthcare startup that needed to scale from 1,000 to 100,000 users in six months. Everyone said it was impossible. We made it happen in four.

Today, we're not just building software—we're crafting the digital backbone for tomorrow's most ambitious companies. Every line of code we write, every system we architect, every problem we solve is guided by one unwavering mission: to turn visionary ideas into reality, faster and more beautifully than anyone thought possible.
