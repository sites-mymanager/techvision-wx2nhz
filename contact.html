Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with TechVision

What types of projects do you work on?

We specialize in cutting-edge technology solutions including AI/ML implementations, cloud architecture, mobile applications, and digital transformation initiatives. From startups to Fortune 500 companies, we adapt our approach to your unique needs.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. Simple applications might take 6-8 weeks, while comprehensive digital transformation projects can span 6-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Absolutely. We believe in long-term partnerships. Our support packages include monitoring, updates, scaling assistance, and feature enhancements to ensure your solution continues to deliver value as your business grows.

What's your approach to project communication?

Transparency is key to our process. You'll have a dedicated project manager, weekly progress updates, access to our project dashboard, and regular check-ins. We use modern collaboration tools to keep everyone aligned.
